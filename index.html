📷 ESP32-CAM Flasher

Flash the hotspot + live-camera firmware straight from your Android phone (Chrome + USB OTG). Also works in Chrome/Edge on a PC.

1Plug in the board

  1. Put the ESP32-CAM on its ESP32-CAM-MB USB base.
  2. Connect it to your phone with a USB OTG adapter (USB-C → USB-A) and a micro-USB cable.
  3. Hold IO0 on the base, tap RST, then let go of IO0. That puts it in flash mode.

2Pick the firmware

Choose firmware.bin, or the firmware.zip you downloaded from GitHub (no need to unzip).

4Watch the camera

  1. Unplug the board from the phone, plug it into a phone charger or power bank, press RST.
  2. Phone Wi-Fi → connect to ESP32-CAM, password 12345678. If Android says "no internet", tap stay connected.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 in Chrome.
